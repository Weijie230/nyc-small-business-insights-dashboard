About Data

Dataset Last Updated: September 7, 2026

This dashboard uses the NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP) Issued Licenses dataset from NYC Open Data.

The processed dataset contains 46,387 license records across the five New York City boroughs. Of these, 29,134 records have Active status.

The dataset includes business names, business categories, license statuses, boroughs, initial issuance dates, and geographic coordinates.

Note: DCWP-issued licenses do not represent every small business in New York City. One business may also have multiple license records.

Methodology

1. Data Collection

License records were obtained from the NYC Open Data DCWP Issued Licenses dataset.

2. Data Cleaning

Python and pandas were used to select relevant fields, process date values, and prepare NYC license records with geographic coordinates for analysis.

3. Data Analysis

The dashboard calculates license record counts, distinct businesses using Business Unique ID, category distributions, borough distributions, and initial issuance year trends.

4. Data Visualization

Chart.js is used for interactive charts, while Leaflet is used to display geographic locations. JavaScript filters the records by borough, business category, and license status.

5. Limitations
Data Limitations:
  • The dataset does not include every business operating in New York City.
  • One business may have multiple license records.
  • Initial license issuance dates do not necessarily represent business opening dates.
  • The 2026 data represents a partial year.
  • The dashboard may not reflect the latest business license changes.

Data Source: NYC Open Data / DCWP Issued Licenses
